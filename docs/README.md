# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview

OctoAcme uses a structured, customer-first approach to project management centered on iterative delivery, clear ownership, and data-informed decisions. This documentation provides guidance for all team members on how we plan, execute, and improve our projects.

## Project Management Approach

OctoAcme follows a comprehensive lifecycle approach that spans five key phases: **Initiation, Planning, Execution, Release, and Close & Retrospective**. The organization emphasizes customer-first delivery through iterative increments, with clear ownership assigned to named Project Managers and Product Leads.

During **Initiation**, teams validate business need and create a lightweight Project One-pager defining the problem, goals, success metrics, and stakeholder alignment. The **Planning phase** breaks approved work into prioritized, estimated backlog items with clear acceptance criteria and a defined Definition of Done. This structured foundation ensures all stakeholders understand scope, dependencies, and timelines before execution begins.

**Core roles and communication** form the backbone of OctoAcme's execution model. Three primary personas drive project success: **Developers** who design and build features while maintaining high test coverage, **Product Managers** who define what should be built and prioritize based on customer value and metrics, and **Project Managers** who coordinate delivery, manage risks, and maintain stakeholder alignment. The team rhythm includes daily standups (15 minutes), weekly delivery syncs, and monthly stakeholder updates. Regular demonstrations occur at sprint or milestone completion, and a formal weekly sync occurs between PM and Product Lead to address dependencies, risks, and decisions.

**Quality and execution practices** emphasize iterative delivery with small pull requests (≤400 lines), automated CI/CD pipelines including tests, linting, and security scanning, and multi-level blocker escalation. The organization maintains a Risk Register capturing impact, likelihood, owner, and mitigation strategies, reviewed weekly. The **Release & Deployment process** enforces pre-release requirements including passing CI/security scans, documented rollback plans, and smoke testing in staging before production deployment. Finally, **continuous improvement** is institutionalized through post-sprint and post-release retrospectives that capture learnings and drive action items, transforming tacit knowledge into documented processes while maintaining psychological safety.

## Core Principles

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has named leads with defined responsibilities
- **Data-informed**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback and learning

## Communication Cadence

- **Daily**: Team standups (15 minutes) focusing on progress, blockers, and dependencies
- **Weekly**: Delivery sync with status updates and risk review
- **Weekly**: PM + Product Lead alignment on priorities and dependencies
- **Monthly**: Stakeholder updates and roadmap reviews
- **Ad-hoc**: Escalations for critical blockers or decisions
